## Decisions

- Software status: **PASS** (replicate machinery, frozen discovery sets,
  shared-G verification, count-unit endpoints all hold across 24 runs).
- Scientific adoption: **DROP** — no worthwhile benefit in any tested regime
  (including the imbalanced target) with no offsetting harm either; the
 /default remains proportional sampling. Scope: DEVELOPMENT simulation,
  24 donors, fixed rank, matched budget. Real-data scale (hundreds of donors,
  where imbalance bites hardest) is explicitly untested — the DROP does not
  extend there, and neither does any positive claim.

## Supported / unsupported scope

Supported: at 24-donor DEVELOPMENT scale, equal-per-donor discovery is
interchangeable with proportional discovery on prediction, recovery, usage
and cost; the draw contributes ~0.2% variance. Unsupported: 100/110 and
selected-rank B (omitted — would measure dropped-A's harm, not B); rare-program
preservation as a bound claim; any real-data statement.
